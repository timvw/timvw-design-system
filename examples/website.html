A few things to know.

Is this a real paid product?

No. Studio is a fictional product used to demonstrate website patterns. The plan prices and customer story are illustrative.

Can I try the workspace?

Yes. The workspace demo runs in your browser with sample projects. Changes are saved locally when browser storage is available.

Can I reuse this layout?

Yes. Copy the HTML and CSS and retain the license and attribution. Adapt the content to your own product.
